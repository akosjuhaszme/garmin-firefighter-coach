body_parts.json contains SVG path data taken from react-native-body-highlighter
(files assets/bodyFront.ts, assets/bodyBack.ts, assets/bodyFemaleFront.ts and assets/bodyFemaleBack.ts,
downloaded 2026-10-14):
https://github.com/HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter

The upstream project does not say where the original artwork of these shapes comes from, so the
provenance of the drawing itself is not documented there. The shapes are redistributed here under the
MIT licence of the upstream project, as that licence permits.
